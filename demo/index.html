How it works

From a region of interest to a time series of morphological change.

2

Water & river extraction

Spectral water indices

Delineate open water from optical imagery with normalized-difference indices, then threshold to a binary water mask.

MNDWI = (Green − SWIR) / (Green + SWIR)
NDWI = (Green − NIR) / (Green + NIR)

River extraction

Connected-component filtering

Label the water mask into connected regions and retain the channel network, discarding spurious patches and isolated ponds by area.

keep component C if area(C) ≥ Amin  ∧  C ∈ channel set
Read the theory →
Curvilinear detection

Modified Multiscale Singularity Index

Detect channels of any width as curvilinear structures (Isikdogan et al., 2015), yielding centerline, orientation, and width without manual masking.

ψ(x,y,σ) = |f0·f2| / (1 + |f1|)  over scales σn

Intertidal mapping

Yearly intertidal detection

Pixels that are intermittently wet across a year are intertidal. Per-pixel water occurrence is combined with the JRC Global Surface Water layer to separate tidal flats from permanent water and land.

occurrence = wet obs / valid obs  —  intertidal where 0 < occurrence < 1

Persistence

Transition matrix & Markov chain

Track how intertidal classes persist or convert year to year, and project lifespan with a first-order Markov model.

Pij = nij / Σj nij  ·  πt+1 = πt P

Channel dynamics

Centerline & bank migration

Extract centerlines and quantify channel and bank migration rates from the lateral displacement of lines between epochs.

rate = Δposition / Δtime  (m yr⁻¹)
